excerpt: 'This thesis develops a nuclear safeguards monitoring approach for a generic Molten Salt Reactor (MSR) using MCNP neutronics modeling. The methodology establishes relationships between plutonium content and key fission product radionuclides (137Cs, 134Cs, 154Eu) to enable Special Nuclear Material (SNM) mass quantification via Non-Destructive Assay. The proposed safeguards approach provides early detection capability to prevent plutonium diversion for non-peaceful purposes.'
